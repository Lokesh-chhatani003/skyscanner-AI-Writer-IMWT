# IT Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical Requirements

- **Formality**: **Always use the informal "tu"** for all user communication. Avoid bureaucratic language.
- **Helpful Priority**: If forced to choose between Playful, Geeky, or Helpful, **prioritize Helpful**.
- **Placeholders**: **Do not translate or delete**. If a location placeholder causes grammatical issues, use: `<location>: [translated text]` or `[text] <location>`.
- **URLs**: **No spaces, no special characters, all lowercase, use hyphens**.
- **Sustainability**: **Never use absolute terms** (e.g., "ecologico", "green"). Use comparatives only, supported by data. Avoid greenwashing imagery.
- **Discounts**: **Never claim "Skyscanner offers" a discount**. Use passive/neutral phrasing: "You are offered a discount".

---

## Tone of Voice: Geeky Travel Pal

**Core Qualities**: Playful, Geeky, Helpful.

- **Playful**: Conversational, lively rhythm. Avoid "fake-smart" corporate speak and "LOL-funny" attempts.
- **Geeky**: Excited to share data/details but human-focused. Break complex info into simple steps.
- **Helpful**: Focus on traveller benefits (saving time/money). 
    - **Apologies**: Drop playfulness. Apologize sincerely once. Focus on solutions. Do not use "We apologize for the inconvenience."
    - **Structure**: Pose question → offer solution. Keep sentences short.

---

## Technical & General Guidelines

### Placeholders & Variables
- **Keep exactly as in source**. 
- Maintain natural order and grammatical correctness.
- Ensure spaces exist before/after tags to prevent word-gluing.

### Inclusive Language
- **Avoid stereotypes** (gender, age, ability, family). 
- Use neutral, precise terms over labels. 
- Do not assume travel companions or ability.

### URL Conventions
- **Format**: Lowercase, hyphen-separated. 
- **Restriction**: No special/extended characters (replace with standard Latin).

### Cultural Sensitivity
- **Emojis/Symbols**: Use only if in source. Ensure market relevance. Never use in legal/serious contexts.
- **Imagery**: Flag culturally inappropriate content (clothing, gestures, food).

---

## Italian Linguistic Guidelines

### Punctuation & Typography
- **Quotes**: Use double straight quotes (`" "`). Place periods **outside** the quotes.
- **Semicolons**: Avoid; use only in very long sentences.
- **Exclamation/Question Marks**: **Never use more than one**.
- **Parentheses**: Replace hyphens with commas or parentheses.
- **Apostrophes**: Use straight apostrophes ('), never curly.
- **Ellipsis**: Use sparingly (informal only). If replacing mid-sentence, no capital letter follows. If replacing end-of-sentence, capitalize the following word.
- **Ampersands**: Use only in brand/company names.

### Euphonic "d"
- Use only between identical vowels: *ad Atene*, *ed ecco*.
- **Exceptions**: *ad esempio*, *ad eccezione di*.
- **Avoid**: Never use "od".

### Formatting & Titles
- **Emphasis**: Use bold/italics. **Avoid ALL CAPS** (perceived as shouting).
- **Titles/Headers**: 
    - **No period at end**. 
    - **Sentence case**: Only first word capitalized (plus proper nouns/brands).
    - **Days/Months**: Lowercase unless specific events (e.g., *Venerdì santo*).

### Dates & Numbers
- **Format**: DD/MM/YYYY.
- **Time**: 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).

### Abbreviations
- Spell out full form on first appearance, followed by abbreviation in parentheses.
- Keep periods in standard abbreviations (*ecc.*, *pag.*, *cap.*).

### Brand & Terminology
- **Skyscanner**: Keep as is. Do not translate. No articles.
- **Airport Names**: **Never translate the word "Airport"** (it is added automatically via placeholder).
- **Anglicisms**: Use Italian equivalent if widespread/accepted.
- **Feature Names**: Focus on clarity over literal translation. Ensure it sounds natural to local users.

---

## Content-Specific Guidelines

### User Interface (UI)
- **Be concise**. One idea per string. Use consistent terminology (e.g., always "Avvisi di prezzo").

### Marketing
- Creative, fluid, natural. Focus on traveller benefits (saving money/time). Avoid being too close to English source.

### SEO
- Natural language. Avoid keyword stuffing. Use clear, descriptive headings.

### Google Ads
- **Very concise**. Direct CTA. **No clickbait**. No superlatives (no "best", "cheapest").

### App Store
- Highlight benefits (search, compare, track). No guarantees/superlatives.

### Legal/Policies
- **Prioritize accuracy/clarity**. No jokes, no emotional language, no interpretation.

---

## Prohibited & Restricted Language

### Superlatives
- **Avoid "best"** unless specific disclaimer explains how it is measured. Use subjective alternatives like "epic prices" or "amazing deals".

### Sustainability (Greenwashing)
- **Do not use**: *sostenibile, ecologica, green, pulita, rispettosa del pianeta*.
- **Prefer**: Comparatives ("X emits 20% less carbon than Y").
- **Constraint**: Only reference performance if verifiable data exists.

---

## Quick Reference: Do vs. Don't

- **Style**:
    - **Do**: "Con Skyscanner puoi confrontare..."
    - **Don't**: "Non serve essere degli esperti..." (Avoid condescending tone).
- **CTA**:
    - **Do**: "Prenota ora"
    - **Don't**: "Prenota Ora" (Avoid Capitalized Words).
- **Clarity**:
    - **Do**: "Scopri le destinazioni che hai visitato..."
    - **Don't**: "Scopri le location..." (Use Italian).
- **Formatting**:
    - **Do**: "Torna ai risultati"
    - **Don't**: "TORNA AI RISULTATI" (No all caps).
- **Airport Names**:
    - **Do**: "Paris Charles de Gaulle"
    - **Don't**: "Aeroporto Paris Charles de Gaulle" (Do not add "Aeroporto").
- **Discounts**:
    - **Do**: "Ti mostriamo i prezzi ridotti del 20%."
    - **Don't**: "Ti mostriamo i prezzi che hanno uno sconto del 20%." (Avoid implying Skyscanner offers the discount).
