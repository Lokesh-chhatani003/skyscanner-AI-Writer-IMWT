# Greek (el-GR) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Project-specific exclusions (ticket 2026_8983 — highest priority)
- Ignore place names written in the Latin alphabet. Do not flag, transliterate, translate, or rewrite them.
- Ignore missing accents/tonos in section headings. Do not flag or add those accents.
- The global project-specific proper-name exclusion in the master prompt takes priority over any geo-name guidance.

## Critical requirements
- Use the formal plural `εσείς` / `σας` consistently, with a friendly and helpful tone.
- Prefer active voice and formal imperative CTAs. Clarity takes priority over wordplay.
- Keep Skyscanner unchanged and use the feminine article where required: `η Skyscanner`, never `το Skyscanner`.

## Language and typography
- Use the Greek decimal comma and locale-appropriate punctuation. Use one ellipsis character `…`, straight quotation marks consistently, and no more than one exclamation mark.
- Use sentence case for headings and CTAs.
- Write dates as `16 Μαρτίου 2025` or `16/03/2025`; write time in 24-hour form such as `15:30`.
- Apply standard final-n rules; `δεν` always keeps final `ν`.
- Distinguish interrogative `πού`/`πώς`, comparative `σαν` from attributive `ως`, and indefinite `μια` from numeric `μία`.

## Technical content
- Preserve placeholders, variables, tags, numbers, and product tokens exactly.
- Do not duplicate the word for airport when it is already part of the source name.
- CTA translations must remain buttons/actions; for example, “Cancel” may require the noun `Ακύρωση` depending on UI context.

## Content and compliance
- Use natural SEO language; no keyword stuffing or preference-only rewrites.
- Do not externally fact-check laws, prices, limits, or policies.
- Do not claim Skyscanner provides discounts. Use “best” only with a supplied measurable basis.
- Avoid greenwashing and absolute claims that travel is green, clean, eco-friendly, or sustainable.
