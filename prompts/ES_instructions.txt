# es-ES Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical Requirements
**Tone**: Always maintain the "Geeky Travel Pal" persona. Prioritize **Helpful** > **Geeky** > **Playful**.
**Formality**: Use informal **"tú"** for all UI, marketing, and help content. Use **"usted"** only in legal/policy texts.
**Accuracy**: Never claim Skyscanner offers a "discount." Discounts come from partners; use "You are offered a discount" or neutral phrasing.
**Placeholders**: **Never translate, delete, or change** placeholders (`%{variable}`, `@@tag@@`). Keep exact syntax; ensure grammatical agreement (use workarounds if necessary).
**Sustainability**: **Avoid greenwashing**. Use comparatives (e.g., "emits less") rather than absolute superlatives (e.g., "eco-friendly," "green").

---

## Tone of Voice: "Geeky Travel Pal"
Our voice must be playful, geeky (data-driven but human), and helpful.

*   **Conversational**: Write like a friend. Avoid stiff/corporate language.
*   **Empathetic**: When delivering bad news, drop playfulness. Apologize sincerely if our fault; show empathy if not. Avoid "Sentimos las molestias."
*   **Simple**: Use short sentences. Focus on traveler benefits (save time/money/effort).
*   **Playful Tools**: Use rhymes, metaphors, or alliteration only when natural. **Never** force it; clarity > wordplay.
*   **Avoid**: "Fake-smart" corporate speak, "LOL-funny" attempts, and over-excitement (prudent use of exclamation marks).

---

## Localization & Translation Strategy
*   **Naturalness**: Adapt expressions to Spanish culture; do not translate word-for-word.
*   **Human Touch**: If an English idiom doesn't work, discard it. Maintain the "smart, friendly travel pal" persona in every sentence.
*   **Inclusive Language**: Avoid stereotypes (gender, age, ability, family). Use neutral terms.
*   **Anglicisms**: Avoid unless widely accepted (e.g., brand names). Prefer local equivalents.
*   **Active Voice**: Mandatory for headlines and CTAs.

---

## Technical Specifications & URL Rules

### URL Requirements
*   **Format**: Lowercase only.
*   **Separators**: Use standard hyphens (`-`) to separate words. No spaces.
*   **Characters**: Latin characters only.
*   **Restrictions**: No special/extended characters.

### Numbers & Formatting
*   **Format**: Use **DD/MM/YYYY**.
*   **Time**: 24-hour format (e.g., `14:00`). Use colon as separator. Do not use "h" unless necessary; if used, insert non-breaking space (e.g., `14:00 h`).
*   **Separators**:
    *   Thousands: Non-breaking space (e.g., `10 000`).
    *   Decimals: Comma (e.g., `10,50`).
    *   Four-figure numbers: No separator (e.g., `1200`).
*   **Non-breaking spaces**: Mandatory between number and symbol/unit (e.g., `20 €`).

### Punctuation
*   **Quotes**: Use `«angular quotation marks»` instead of English “ ”.
*   **Sentence Case**: Use for titles and CTAs. Only capitalize first word and proper nouns.
*   **Exclamation Marks**: Use sparingly.
*   **Full Stops**: Do not use in buttons, menus, headers, or titles.
*   **Colons**: No capital letter after colon, except for greetings, "Nota:", "Aviso:", or quotes.

---

## Content Type Guidelines

### UI
*   **Goal**: Short, clear, action-oriented.
*   **Consistency**: Use standard terms across the product (e.g., always "Alertas de precios").
*   **CTAs**: Use active voice. Infinitive form for user-led actions (e.g., "Reservar"); Imperative when Skyscanner encourages action.

### Marketing
*   **Tone**: Inspirational, natural, fluid.
*   **Focus**: Benefits (saving, discovery).
*   **Avoid**: Unprovable claims ("#1", "cheapest ever").

### SEO
*   **Constraint**: No keyword stuffing. Write for humans.
*   **Formatting**: Clear, descriptive headings. Concise meta titles.

### Legal & Policies
*   **Tone**: Precise, neutral, formal ("usted").
*   **Rule**: No jokes, idioms, or emotional language.

---

## Terminology & Product Constraints

### Skyscanner Brand
*   **Usage**: Never translate the name "Skyscanner".
*   **Articles**: Do not add articles unless linguistically mandatory.

### Feature Naming
*   **Focus**: User clarity.
*   **Verification**: Check against local competitors. Avoid literal translation if misleading (e.g., "Packages" → use local equivalent like "vacaciones" if referring to flight+hotel).

### "Best" & Superlatives
*   **Rule**: Do not use "best" / "best deals" as an objective claim.
*   **Exception**: Only use if supported by a clear, visible disclaimer explaining the methodology. If English says "best" but no disclaimer exists, use subjective alternatives ("precios increíbles," "ofertas fantásticas").

### Sustainability Content
*   **Principles**:
    *   Use comparatives (e.g., "X is more sustainable than Y").
    *   **Prohibited**: Absolute terms like "green," "eco," "clean," "planet-friendly," "guilt-free."
    *   **Data**: Base claims on measurable, standardized criteria.

## SEO / Editorial LQA Addendum (Jan 2026)
Source: Style Guide Jan 2026 (Spanish ES). Additive.

### Register (mandatory)
- Informal **tú** for UI/marketing/SEO/help. Use **usted** only in legal/policy. Flag wrong register as **Compliance**.
- Refer to Skyscanner as **nosotros / nos / nuestro**.

### Geo naming
- Official/common local names; consistent spelling; no over-translation of expected originals (landmarks/airports).

### SEO body readability (objective Style only)
- Split objectively overlong multi-idea SEO body paragraphs (**Style**); provide split text in `updated_sentence` / `corrected_content`.
- No synonym-only preference edits.
