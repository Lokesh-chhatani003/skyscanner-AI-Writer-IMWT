# NL Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical Requirements
- **Tone Hierarchy**: **Helpful > Geeky > Playful**. If a rule conflicts, be helpful first.
- **Formality**: Use informal **'je/jij'** consistently across all content.
- **Brand Name**: **Never translate 'Skyscanner'**. Do not add articles.
- **'Discount' Forbidden**: **Never** refer to 'discount' (korting). Only partners offer discounts. Use 'deals' or 'price drops'.
- **'Best' Claims**: Only use 'best' if accompanied by a disclaimer explaining the measurement.
- **Sustainability**: Avoid greenwashing. Use comparatives only (e.g., 'more sustainable'), never absolutes (e.g., 'green', 'eco').
- **No Em-dashes**: Never use em-dashes (—). Use en-dashes (–) for side notes or commas/brackets.

## Tone of Voice: The Geeky Travel Pal
Our tone is **playful, geeky, and helpful**.
- **Playful**: Light, natural energy. Use rhyme/alliteration/metaphors sparingly if natural. Avoid stiff corporate speak or over-the-top humor.
- **Geeky**: Enthusiastic about data/patterns. Explain complex info simply.
- **Helpful**: Foundation of our brand. Clear, honest, empathetic.
- **Apologies**: If our fault, apologize sincerely once. Focus on solutions. Do not use cold phrases like "Onze excuses voor het ongemak."

## Writing & Technical Guidelines
- **Active Voice**: Use everywhere, especially in CTAs and headlines.
- **Conciseness**: Short sentences. Remove unnecessary words/repetition.
- **Placeholders/Tags**: **Do not translate, delete, or change** tags (e.g., `@@city@@`). Keep exact order, spacing, and grammar.
- **URLs**:
    - Format: Lowercase, hyphenated (no spaces).
    - Encoding: **Latin characters only**. No special characters (e.g., replace 'ë' with 'e').
- **Inclusive Language**: Avoid stereotypes (gender, age, ability, background). Use neutral, precise terms.
- **Geographic Terms**: Use official/widely used local names. **Never add 'luchthaven' to airport names** (avoid duplication).
- **Anglicisms**: Avoid unless widely expected (e.g., brand names).

## Dutch Punctuation & Formatting
- **Commas**: Pause-based. Do not use Oxford commas.
- **Ellipsis**: Space before and after, unless breaking a word. No full stop after ellipsis at sentence end.
- **Exclamation Marks**: **Use sparingly**. Avoid 'shouty' tone.
- **Quotation Marks**: Double straight for quotes. Single straight for UI elements/thoughts.
- **Full Stops**: Standard rules apply. **Do not use in titles, headers, or buttons**.
- **Emphasis**: Use **accent aigu** only. Avoid ALL CAPS and excessive bolding.
- **Capitalization**:
    - **Sentence case preferred** for UI/Marketing.
    - Months/days: Lowercase in running text (only cap if first word/standalone).
    - Colon: First letter after colon is lowercase unless it's a name, full sentence, or quote.
- **Abbreviations**: Only if widely understood (e.g., airport codes). Define first, then abbreviate.

## Content-Specific Rules
- **UI**: Short, clear, action-focused. One idea per string. Consistent terminology.
- **Marketing**: Natural, fluid, inspirational, compliant. Focus on benefits.
- **SEO**: Natural language, descriptive headers. Avoid keyword stuffing.
- **Google Ads**: Concise, clear benefits, direct CTA. Avoid unprovable superlatives (e.g., 'nummer 1').
- **Editorial**: Warm, descriptive, factual.
- **Legal/Policies**: Precise, neutral. No jokes/emotions. No added interpretations.
- **Emojis**: Use sparingly. Avoid suggestive/culturally inappropriate ones. No emojis in legal/policy/incident content.

## Sustainability Content
- **Comparatives Only**: Use 'X is more sustainable than Y'.
- **No Absolutes**: Avoid 'ecological', 'green', 'clean', 'planet-friendly', 'guilt-free'.
- **Evidence-Based**: Only reference environmental performance if data is available.

## Glossary & Terminology Management
- **Feature Names**: Prioritize user clarity over literal translation. Must be consistent with local travel provider norms.
- **UI Elements**: Use single, straight quotes for UI elements (e.g., 'Terug naar resultaten').
- **Dates/Days**:
    - Months: jan, feb, mrt, apr, mei, jun, jul, aug, sep, okt, nov, dec
    - Days: ma, di, wo, do, vr, za, zo

## Do & Don't Summary
- **Do**: "Eindelijk een manier om vluchten te vergelijken..."
- **Don't**: Use "TOT. OP. DE. GRAM." or multiple exclamation marks.
- **Do**: "Vergelijk vluchten naar Tokio en bespaar tijd."
- **Don't**: Make unsubstantiated claims like "Gegarandeerd de goedkoopste".
- **Do**: Use "Belasting toegevoegde waarde (btw)" on first mention, then "btw".
- **Don't**: Use internal acronyms without explanation (e.g., "PNR").
- **Do**: Use active, concise CTAs (e.g., "Nu boeken").
- **Don't**: Use passive/vague phrases (e.g., "Klaar om te boeken?").
- **Do**: Use the correct equivalent for "deal".
- **Don't**: Mix English/local words to sound "trendy".


## SEO / Editorial LQA Addendum (Jan 2026)
Source: Style Guide Jan 2026 (Dutch). Additive.

### Register (mandatory)
- Informal **je / jij** (not *u*) for standard product/SEO. Flag *u* as **Compliance** outside legal exceptions.
- CTAs in infinitive where applicable.

### Geo naming
- Always use correct Dutch geographical equivalents; never duplicate “luchthaven/airport” in placeholders.

### SEO body readability (objective Style only)
- Split objectively overlong multi-idea SEO body paragraphs (**Style**); provide split text in `updated_sentence` / `corrected_content`.
- No synonym-only preference edits.
