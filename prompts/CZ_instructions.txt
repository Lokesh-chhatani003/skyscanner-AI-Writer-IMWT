# Czech (cs-CZ) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical requirements
- Use a friendly but formal V-form consistently. Write `vám`, `váš`, and `vás` in lowercase except at the start of a sentence.
- Prefer gender-neutral wording. Use slash forms only when no clear neutral alternative exists.
- Helpful > geeky > playful. Keep language direct, active, concise, and free of corporate jargon.
- The global project-specific exclusion in the master prompt takes priority over any geo-name guidance.

## Language and typography
- Use Czech quotation marks `„…“` and the ellipsis character `…`. Avoid excessive exclamation marks.
- Use sentence case for headings and CTAs. Days and months are lowercase.
- Use a non-breaking space where Czech requires it: between a number and unit or percent sign, in large numbers, and after a one-letter preposition. Never output the literal text `&nbsp;`.
- Write dates as `12. května 2025` or `12. 5. 2025` according to context.
- Keep list punctuation and capitalization consistent.

## Currency and units (home market: Czechia)
- Home-market currency: the Czech koruna, written after the value with a non-breaking space (`250 Kč`). Use it for every price whatever the destination, never the destination's own currency. `$45 za den` for a US destination → **Accuracy · Major** → `950 Kč za den`.
- Home-market measurement system: metric (km, m, °C, l, kg). Convert destination measurements to metric and show only the metric value.
- Exception — speed limits and other posted road signage at the destination: keep the destination's unit first, then the metric value in parentheses, `100 mph (přibližně 160 km/h)`. A speed limit shown in only one unit, or with km/h first, → **Compliance · Major**.

## Technical content
- Use short, active imperative CTAs in the formal register.
- Preserve placeholders, variables, tags, numbers, and product tokens exactly.
- Do not duplicate `letiště` when the source airport name already contains it.
- Keep Skyscanner unchanged; ordinary Czech inflection is acceptable only when grammatically required.

## Content and compliance
- Use natural SEO language; no keyword stuffing or preference-only rewrites.
- Do not externally fact-check laws, prices, limits, or policies.
- Do not claim Skyscanner provides discounts; keep price/offer wording neutral.
- Use “best” only with a supplied measurable basis. Avoid absolute sustainability or green claims.
