# Hungarian (hu-HU) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical requirements
- Use an informal, friendly `tegeződés` register consistently. Keep wording direct, active, concise, and helpful.
- Helpful > geeky > playful; avoid corporate language, forced jokes, and excessive exclamation marks.
- The global project-specific exclusion in the master prompt takes priority over any name or geo-name guidance.

## Language and typography
- Use Hungarian quotation marks `„…”`. Do not put a comma after `Szia`.
- Use `…` without a preceding space. Avoid em dashes; use the correct hyphen or closed en dash for ranges and routes.
- Use non-breaking spaces between numbers and units where required, but no space before `%`. Never output the literal text `&nbsp;`.
- Use sentence case and do not end headings with a full stop.
- Write dates in Hungarian order, for example `2025. március 25.`.
- After a colon, use lowercase unless a complete sentence, quotation, or proper name follows.

## Currency and units (home market: Hungary)
- Home-market currency: the forint, written after the value with a non-breaking space (`16 000 Ft`). Use it for every price whatever the destination, never the destination's own currency. `$45 naponta` for a US destination → **Accuracy · Major** → `16 000 Ft naponta`.
- Home-market measurement system: metric (km, m, °C, liter, kg). Convert destination measurements to metric and show only the metric value.
- Exception — speed limits and other posted road signage at the destination: keep the destination's unit first, then the metric value in parentheses, `100 mph (kb. 160 km/h)`. A speed limit shown in only one unit, or with km/h first, → **Compliance · Major**.

## Technical content
- Use short active CTAs appropriate to the informal register.
- Preserve placeholders, variables, tags, numbers, and product tokens exactly.
- Do not duplicate the airport word when it is already present in the source name.
- Keep Skyscanner unchanged.

## Content and compliance
- Use natural SEO language; no keyword stuffing or preference-only rewrites.
- Do not externally fact-check laws, prices, limits, or policies.
- Do not claim Skyscanner provides discounts. Use “best” only with a supplied measurable basis.
- Avoid greenwashing and absolute claims that travel is green, clean, eco-friendly, or sustainable.
