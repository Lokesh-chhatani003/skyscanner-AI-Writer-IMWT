# Polish (pl-PL) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical requirements
- Address the reader directly, but do not use `Pan`/`Pani`. Capitalize direct-address pronouns such as `Ty`, `Tobie`, `Ciebie`, `Wy`, `Wam`, and `Was`.
- Prefer gender-neutral constructions and keep the form of address consistent.
- Use active voice and short imperative CTAs. Helpful > geeky > playful.
- The global project-specific exclusion in the master prompt takes priority over any name or geo-name guidance.

## Language and typography
- Use Polish quotation marks `„…”` and the ellipsis character `…`.
- Use an en dash, not an em dash, for parenthetical breaks and ranges where appropriate.
- Use sentence case; do not end headings with a full stop. Avoid excessive exclamation marks.
- Keep list capitalization and punctuation consistent.

## Technical content
- Preserve placeholders, variables, tags, numbers, and product tokens exactly.
- Do not duplicate the airport word when it is already part of the source name.
- Keep Skyscanner unchanged.

## Content and compliance
- Use natural SEO language; no keyword stuffing or preference-only rewrites.
- Do not externally fact-check laws, prices, limits, or policies.
- Do not claim Skyscanner provides discounts. Use neutral wording about offers and price reductions.
- Use “best” only with a supplied measurable basis.
- Avoid greenwashing and absolute claims that travel is green, clean, eco-friendly, or sustainable.
