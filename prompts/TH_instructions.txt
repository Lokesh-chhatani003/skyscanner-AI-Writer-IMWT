# Thai (th-TH) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical requirements
- Use polite, respectful, concise Thai. Use `คุณ` and polite particles only where natural and keep the register consistent.
- Helpful > geeky > playful. Avoid stiff literal translation, slang, forced jokes, and excessive emphasis.
- The injected project instructions take priority over any name or transliteration guidance.

## Language and typography
- Use natural Thai spacing between clauses and ideas; do not insert spaces between every word.
- Do not add a full stop at the end of Thai headings, complete sentences, paragraphs, bullets, or CTAs.
- Do not add punctuation to CTA labels. Use a decimal point for decimal numbers.
- Apply sentence case only to Latin-script text. Avoid repeated Thai characters used only for emphasis.
- Keep quotation marks and referenced UI labels consistent with the supplied text.

## Currency and units (home market: Thailand)
- Home-market currency: the baht (`฿` before the value, or `บาท` after it — follow the pattern already used in the text). Use it for every price whatever the destination, never the destination's own currency. A US-dollar price in th-TH content → **Accuracy · Major**, converted to baht.
- Home-market measurement system: metric (กม., ม., °C, ลิตร, กก.). Convert destination measurements to metric and show only the metric value.
- Exception — speed limits and other posted road signage at the destination: keep the destination's unit first, then the metric value in parentheses, `100 mph (ประมาณ 160 กม./ชม.)`. A speed limit shown in only one unit, or with the metric value first, → **Compliance · Major**.

## Technical content
- Preserve placeholders, variables, tags, numbers, and product tokens exactly. A placeholder may move for Thai grammar, but its characters must not change.
- Do not insert an unnecessary space between a short Thai particle and its placeholder.
- Follow the source’s calendar system and date meaning; do not convert eras without explicit evidence.
- Do not duplicate the airport word when it is already part of the source name.
- Keep Skyscanner unchanged.

## Content and compliance
- Use natural SEO language; no keyword stuffing or preference-only rewrites.
- Do not externally fact-check laws, prices, limits, or policies.
- Do not claim Skyscanner provides discounts. Use “best” only with a supplied measurable basis.
- Avoid greenwashing and absolute claims that travel is green, clean, eco-friendly, or sustainable.
