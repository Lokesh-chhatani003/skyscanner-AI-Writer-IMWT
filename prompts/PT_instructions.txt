# European Portuguese (pt-PT) Style Guide for Skyscanner

## Critical Requirements
**Tone of Voice**: Be a **Geeky Travel Pal**. Tone must be **playful, geeky, and helpful**.
- **Hierarchy**: **Helpful > Geeky > Playful**. If forced to choose, be helpful first.
- **Playful**: Conversational and lively. Clarity always beats wordplay.
- **Geeky**: Enthusiastic about data and patterns, explained like a knowledgeable friend.
- **Helpful**: Make complex travel info simple; put travelers first.
- **Avoid**: Corporate jargon, stiff phrasing, forced jokes, excessive exclamation marks.

## Formality (mandatory — pt-PT)
- Use the **formal 3rd-person** register for all communications.
- **Do not use "você"** and **do not use informal "tu"** (nor *tua/teu/tuas/teus*, nor 2nd-person verb forms like *encontras*, *reservares*, *alugares*).
- Prefer impersonal / 3rd-person constructions (e.g. *é necessário*, *recomendamos*, *o utilizador*, *pode*).
- Flag any *tu* / *você* register leakage as **Compliance** (Major unless a single isolated slip).

## Linguistic Guidelines

### Spelling & Orthography
- Follow **European Portuguese** spelling (Acordo Ortográfico as used in Portugal).
- Prefer European lexicon over Brazilian variants when both appear (e.g. *autocarro* not *ônibus*, *pequeno-almoço* not *café da manhã*, *telemóvel* not *celular*, *ecrã* not *tela*, where context is Portugal).
- Flag clear BR-only forms in pt-PT market content as **Compliance** / **Accuracy** when they are objectively wrong for Portugal.

### Punctuation & Spacing
- Use European Portuguese punctuation conventions.
- Currency: non-breaking space between value and symbol where the locale requires it (e.g. `52 €`).
- Prefer clarity: commas where needed to separate clauses.

### Capitalization & Dates
- **Sentence case** for titles and CTAs.
- Dates in Portuguese long form when written out (`11 de janeiro de 2026`).

### Addressing Users & CTAs
- **Active voice** for headlines and CTAs.
- CTAs in the **imperative** (short, direct), consistent with formal register (not *tu* imperatives).

### Units
- Metric-first market. Missing mandated metric presentation when only imperial is shown → **Compliance**.

## Technical Requirements

### URLs
- All lowercase, hyphens for word separation, basic Latin characters only.

### Placeholders
- Never translate, delete, or alter placeholders. Keep spacing around tags.

### Airport Names
- Never add "aeroporto" if it is not in the source name (avoid duplication).

### Geo-terms
- Use official / most widely used Portuguese names. EU style-guide country list is a valid reference.

## Content Type Guidelines

### SEO / Editorial
- Natural language, no keyword stuffing. Clear descriptive headings.
- If one SEO body paragraph packs multiple distinct ideas and is objectively hard to scan, flag **Style** and split into shorter paragraphs in `updated_sentence` / `corrected_content`.

### Marketing
- Natural, fluid, inspirational; keep promises realistic and compliant.

### Legal / Policies
- Precise, neutral, unemotional. No jokes or idioms.

### Sustainability
- No greenwashing. Prefer comparatives over absolutes. Never claim a flight is "sustentável/verde/eco/limpo".

## Brand & Legal Constraints

### Skyscanner
- Never translate or alter the brand name "Skyscanner".

### "Best" & Superlatives
- Do not claim "melhores preços" / "melhores ofertas" as an unsubstantiated competitive claim.
- "Best" allowed only with a clear measurement disclaimer.

### "Discount" & "Deals"
- Skyscanner never offers discounts. Never "A Skyscanner ofereceerece um desconto".
- Prefer neutral phrasing (*ofertas*, *redução de preço*, *é-lhe oferecido um desconto*).

## Quick Reference: Dos and Don'ts

| Category | Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Register** | Formal 3rd person / impersonal | *tu*, *você*, *encontras*, *reservares* |
| **Lexicon (PT)** | *autocarro*, *telemóvel* | BR-only *ônibus*, *celular* (in PT-PT market) |
| **Currency** | `52 €` | `€52` / glued forms that break locale rules |
| **Airport** | Keep source airport name | Duplicate "Aeroporto Aeroporto …" |
| **Discounts** | *ofertas*, *redução de preço* | "A Skyscanner ofereceerece desconto" |
| **Sustainability** | "emite 20% menos carbono" | "voo sustentável/verde" |
